Attribution was wired directly into the practice management system—so every ad click could be tied to a booked appointment within 72 hours."
outcome: "Within the first 60 days, cost-per-acquisition dropped from $340 to $163. Booked new-patient appointments increased by 217 per cent quarter-over-quarter on a budget that was actually 18 per cent lower than the prior period. Meridian has since expanded the programme to two additional clinic locations and is on track to reduce their acquisition cost below $100 by end of year."
