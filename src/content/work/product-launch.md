title: "Product Launch Site"
client: "Vanta Instruments"
tagline: "Built the launch web presence for a precision hardware tool going direct-to-consumer for the first time."
services: ["Web Development", "Content Strategy", "Brand Identity"]
problem: "Vanta Instruments had spent four years building a calibration tool used by automotive engineers. They had zero consumer presence—no website, no e-commerce, no content. Their first direct-to-consumer SKU was set to ship in fourteen weeks. The founding team had strong technical credibility but had never written for buyers outside a procurement context. The price point ($1,800) meant the site had to do serious conversion work before anyone picked up a phone."
approach: "We ran a three-day content sprint with the founders to pull out the technical claims that actually mattered to end buyers versus the spec-sheet language they defaulted to. From that we built a messaging architecture: one proof point per page section, nothing unsupported. The site was built on Astro for performance—under 1.2s LCP on mobile. We shot product and process photography in a single day using the founders' existing workshop. Every page was copy-tested against a panel of five target buyers before launch."
outcome: "The site launched with five days to spare. In the first seven days post-launch, 340 units sold—12 per cent above the founder's best-case projection. The site's average session duration (4 minutes 12 seconds) indicated buyers were reading carefully before converting. Vanta has since used the site as the brief for their in-house team, who have maintained it without agency support."
